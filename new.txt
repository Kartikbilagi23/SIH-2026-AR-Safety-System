AR-Based Vocational Training Simulator for Industrial Safety
Proposed Technology Stack & System Working — Hinglish Report
1. Project Overview

Humara project Jharkhand ke mining aur manufacturing sector ke workers ke liye AR-based industrial safety training platform hoga.

Traditional safety training mein workers ko mostly:

classroom lectures
videos
PDFs
posters
theoretical demonstrations

ke through train kiya jata hai.

Hamare system mein worker interactive 3D/AR scenarios ke through safety procedures practice karega.

For example:

Worker ko ek mining/factory environment diya jayega jahan gas leakage, fire, electrical hazard, unsafe equipment, emergency exit jaise situations create hongi.

Worker ko situation identify karke correct action perform karna hoga.

Example:

Gas Leak Detected
       ↓
Identify Hazard
       ↓
Select Correct PPE
       ↓
Avoid Dangerous Zone
       ↓
Find Emergency Exit
       ↓
Trigger Emergency Procedure
       ↓
Assessment
       ↓
Score + Feedback
       ↓
Certificate

9. 🔥 Training Scenario Working

Suppose hum Fire Safety Module bana rahe hain.

Worker application open karta hai:

Fire Safety Training
        ↓
Start Scenario
        ↓
3D / AR Environment
        ↓
Fire Hazard Appears
        ↓
Worker identifies hazard
        ↓
Select PPE
        ↓
Select Fire Extinguisher
        ↓
Locate Emergency Exit
        ↓
Perform Correct Action
        ↓
Scenario Completed

Har action ke basis par score calculate hoga.

Example:

Hazard identified       +20
Correct PPE             +20
Correct extinguisher    +20
Correct evacuation      +30
Unsafe action            -10
--------------------------------
Total                    80/100

Then:

80/100

PASS ✅

Feedback:
Good hazard identification.
Improve evacuation response time.

11. 📱 Offline Functionality

Ye important part hai because industrial/mining locations mein reliable internet har jagah available nahi ho sakta.

Isliye Android application mein required training content locally available rakhenge.

Internet Available
       ↓
Download Training Module
       ↓
Local Device Storage
       ↓
Worker can train offline
       ↓
Score stored locally
       ↓
Internet comes back
       ↓
Sync with Backend

Example:

Local Attempt

attemptId
scenarioId
score
answers
timestamp
syncStatus = PENDING

Internet available hone par:

PENDING
   ↓
POST /api/attempts/sync
   ↓
Backend
   ↓
syncStatus = SYNCED
